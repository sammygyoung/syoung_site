La Jolla and San Diego Recommendations

To Dos in La Jolla

  • La Jolla Cove and Coast Walk Trail: La Jolla Cove is very close to the hotels and offers views of the cliffs, oceans, and sometimes seals. If you walk north along La Jolla Cove from the hotel, you can continue along the Coast Walk Trail for a beautiful short trail overlooking the ocean. Walking south also gives beautiful views of the ocean.
  • La Jolla Shores Beach: The closest sandy beach to hang out on is La Jolla Shores. You can rent kayaks (including guided tours) to explore the cliffs along La Jolla Cove or surfboard.
  • Birch Aquarium at Scripps: The nearby Birch Aquarium features a variety of marine life exhibits.
  • Torrey Pines State Reserve: Torrey Pines State Reserve has beautiful hiking trails along the beach. We recommend the Beach Trail, which takes you down to the beach. Park here for hiking. The Torrey Pines Golf Course is nearby. Finally, you can go to the nearby Torrey Pines Gilderport for ocean views and to watch people paragliding over the ocean.
  • Trilogy Sanctuary Yoga Studio: (Liz Young Recommendation!) Well-known yoga studio that offers sky deck aerial yoga, which is a really fun experience. They also have a great healthy restaurant on the top floor sky deck.

To Dos in San Diego

  • Balboa Park: Balboa Park is one of San Diego’s most famous spots. This beautiful, 12,000-acre park in the heart of San Diego is home to over a dozen museums and the world-famous San Diego Zoo. When you’re done exploring the park, check out the nearby Hillcrest and North Park neighborhoods, which offer many great spots to eat and drink!
  • Gaslamp Quarter (Downtown San Diego): The Gaslamp is the touristy heart of downtown San Diego, known for its nightlife, big restaurants, and proximity to Petco Park. Little Italy is also a fun neighborhood in downtown to explore.
  • USS Midway Museum: The USS Midway Museum is right on the waterfront in downtown San Diego. It’s an aircraft carrier that’s been turned into a museum, with lots of planes and history to check out. Right outside, the Embarcadero area has nice paths for walking, art to see, and lots of places to grab a bite.
  • Pacific Beach, Mission Bay, Ocean Beach, and Sunset Cliffs: Outside of La Jolla, there are several nearby beaches that offer different beach vibes.
    • Just south of La Jolla is Pacific Beach, which you can expect to be packed in the summer! PB has a bustling boardwalk with many bars and restaurants on the edge of the beach. If you’re looking for more of a party scene, PB is definitely your spot!
    • South of Pacific Beach, the coastline continues with Mission Beach and Mission Bay, a narrow strip of land between Mission Bay and the Pacific Ocean, which offers a beautiful beach and boardwalk. At Mission Bay, you can rent equipment for any water activity you can think of, from kayaks to SUPs to jet skis. Mission Bay Park is a massive aquatic park, with 27 miles of shoreline. Perfect spot for picnicking or getting out on the water.
    • Continuing south, Ocean Beach offers a funky beach town vibe. Lounge at the beach, and then grab some food or drink on the town’s busy Newport Ave.
    • Finally, Sunset Cliffs Natural Park is a recreational area along the Point Loma peninsula’s western shoreline and offers some of the most incredible views of the Pacific Ocean. You can walk or drive along the coastline to enjoy the beautiful views.

Where to Eat and Drink in La Jolla + San Diego

  • Taco Stand: Fantastic taco shop and Mexican restaurant in La Jolla. The lines get long, so be prepared.
  • The Cottage: Californian daytime cuisine served on a patio with a white picket fence or inside a vintage home. Good for breakfast and lunch.
  • Dukes: Airy two-story destination for Hawaiian-inspired fare with outdoor seating and ocean views.
  • Caroline’s: great California-style brunch and lunch space on the beach at La Jolla shores. We recommend going there for lunch if you have a beach day at La Jolla shores.
  • George’s At the Cove: higher-end restaurant with an elegant ocean view.
  • Craft Breweries: San Diego has a famous beer craft brewing scene, although there aren’t many in La Jolla. See here for some recommendations.
